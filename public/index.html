STARTER · 3 MINUTES

Recall, then predict.

Try these before reading. Open each prompt when you’re ready to check or compare your idea.

1. What is the relationship between a chromosome and DNA?

A chromosome is made of coiled DNA.

2. What is a gene, and what does it code for?

A gene is a length of DNA that codes for a protein.

3. Predict: how could a change in DNA affect how a cell works?

A changed gene may code for a protein with a different amino-acid sequence or shape, which can affect its function.

PLENARY · EXIT TICKET

Explain it in your own words.

1. How does a gene in the nucleus help make a protein in the cytoplasm?

The gene remains in the nucleus. mRNA copies it, moves to the cytoplasm and passes through a ribosome, where amino acids are assembled in the order specified by the mRNA bases.

2. If most body cells have the same genes, why can they do different jobs?

Different cells express different genes and make the particular proteins they need.

3. What is the difference between a haploid and diploid human nucleus?

Haploid has one set of 23 chromosomes. Diploid has two sets: 23 pairs, or 46 chromosomes.
